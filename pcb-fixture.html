Modular PCB Test Fixture

type Bed-of-Nails Functional Tester pogo pins Spring-Loaded Crown Gold Pins cost reduction 95% vs Commercial Fixtures cad SolidWorks 20+ Custom Parts

A modular, cost-effective bed-of-nails PCB test fixture designed to support rapid prototyping and production verification across multiple telematics board revisions. Integrates quick-swap pogo pin plates, cam-lock lever clamping, and balanced spring closure.

[01/08] How: Modeled in SolidWorks with a removable pogo pin plate and spring-loaded base to accommodate different board sizes. Fabricated the enclosure using 3D printing and Dremeled ABS enclosure. Designed the layout for easy internal wiring access, reducing testing setup time and supporting quick board layout swaps.
  • Engineered a modular test fixture in SolidWorks with interchangeable test-point plates, slashing fixture costs by 95%.
  • Integrated spring-loaded pogo pin alignment plates maintaining < 0.2 mm registration across high-density test pads.
  • Designed an ergonomic dual-action latching mechanism that applies even downward pressure across the DUT without board warping.

01Mechanical Design & CAD Assembly

Comprehensive SolidWorks 3D CAD modeling, multi-body kinematics, tolerance stack-up analysis, and assembly architecture.

01.1 Full Test Fixture Architecture & Enclosure CAD

SolidWorks 3D parametric CAD modeling of the modular testing fixture within an ABS enclosure for rapid, repeatable production validation.

[01/04] Full Assembly: CAD model of the modular PCB fixture.

01.2 Multi-Layer Exploded Assembly & Internal Stack-Up

Exploded assembly view showing PCB plate layers, pogo pin alignment arrays, dual-acting hinge, latch release, spring columns, and hardware stack-up.

[01/05] Assembly Breakdown: Exploded view show PCB plate layers, pogo pin alignment, hinge and lid mechanism, lock and release system, springs, screws, ABS enclosure, and cutouts to illustrate full assembly.

01.3 Parallel-Clamping Lid & Spring Hinge Kinematics

Custom floating hinge mechanism ensuring the lid descends completely parallel onto the PCB surface, preventing uneven probe shear and contact wear.

[01/06] Lid Mechanism: Lid stops parallel above PCB, leaving a gap to press to PCB down flat onto the pogo pins.

01.4 Quick-Release Push-Button Latch Mechanism

Dual spring-loaded latching hooks that snap securely onto chassis retention lips upon lid closure and release instantly via ergonomic side buttons.

[01/03] Lock Mechanism: Spring-loaded latch locks onto the edge when the lid is pressed down. Pressing the buttons moves the latch off the edge, releasing the lid. Allows for easy PCB mounting onto the test probes.

01.5 Swappable Bed-of-Nails Probe Plates & Guides

Two-piece modular cassette architecture: top pressure stubs apply balanced force while the bottom receiver holds spring-loaded pogo pins and guide pins.

[01/06] • PCB Pin Plates: The top plate has stubs that align with PCB gaps to press it down safely as the lid closes. The bottom plate holds the pogo pins and spring columns. Both plates are swappable for different PCB designs.

High-infill FDM printed chassis frames, latch components, and precision Dremel rotary machining of standard ABS enclosures for button cutouts.

Calibrating compression springs from McMaster-Carr to dial in the ideal clamping pressure, and verifying slide tolerances of swappable PCB carriers.

[01/04] • Spring Loaded Parts: Assembled springs into slots to test the hinge and lock mechanism functionality.

Sub-assembly of spring-loaded latch levers, release pushbuttons, and overhead silicone-tipped pressure pins into the ABS lid frame.

Joining upper lid and base enclosure via stainless steel hinge pins and aligning multi-tier internal electronics mounting studs.

[01/02] • Fixture Assembly: Assembled body and lid, connected hinge, and assembled into the ABS enclosure.

[01/05] • Fixture Testing: Tested lid mechanism, PCB mounting, and probe alignment. Built two complete fixtures.
