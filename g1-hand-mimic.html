Hand Mimic — Real-Time Camera-to-Robot-Hand Teleoperation

platform Inspire RH56 6-DOF hand pipeline MediaPipe @ 25–30 Hz latency ~30 ms link RS-485 @ 115200 baud

A human hand is tracked by a webcam, and every finger movement is mirrored live to the robot's dexterous hand — with an annotated skeleton overlay showing exactly what's being sent to hardware.

  • Real-time 21-landmark MediaPipe tracking mirrors finger articulation at ~30 ms latency.
  • Scale-invariant pinch snapping and occlusion guards eliminate landmark loss inside fists.
  • Direct RS-485 serial bus streaming to dual Inspire RH56 hands at 115,200 baud.
