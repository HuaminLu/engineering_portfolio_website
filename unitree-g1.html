Unitree G1 Humanoid — Teleoperation, Imitation & RL Control Suite

platform G1 EDU, 29 DOF hands Inspire RH56 × 2 compute Jetson Orin NX middleware CycloneDDS

Five working control projects on a full-size 29-DOF bipedal humanoid — spanning every control plane of the robot: low-level 50 Hz joint streaming, high-level locomotion RPC, and serial-driven dexterous hands.

  • Trained a PPO policy for ~53M timesteps that solves 7-joint inverse kinematics in real time — no analytical IK solver — then deployed it sim-to-real over DDS.
  • Built a real-time hand teleoperation pipeline (~30 ms latency) mirroring a human hand from a webcam to a 6-DOF Inspire dexterous hand at 25–30 Hz.
  • Root-caused a firmware RPC failure (error 3102) to an SDK service-name mismatch introduced in ai_sport ≥ 8.2.0.0 — a two-line patch restored all locomotion control.
  • Authored robot behaviors by demonstration — recorder GUIs whose keyframed trajectories train continuous MLP policies for the arms.

03The Three Control Planes

The suite deliberately covers every level of abstraction the robot exposes — from owning each joint's torque loop to delegating the entire gait.

  • low-level joint streamingProjects 3 & 4 — publish q, dq, tau, kp, kd per joint at 50 Hz on rt/arm_sdk; you own the motion
  • high-level locomotion RPCProject 5 — send a vx / vy / ω body-velocity setpoint; the onboard controller owns balance and gait
  • serial register controlProjects 1 & 2 — 6-DOF angle integers over RS-485; the hand firmware owns the servo loops

PD law applied by each joint's motor firmware

τ_applied = kp·(q_cmd − q_measured) + kd·(dq_cmd − dq_measured) + τ_ff

Every LowCmd packet carries 30 motor slots (arms 15–28, waist 12–14, slot 29 = arm_sdk enable flag) and ends with a CRC-32 checksum. State feedback arrives on rt/lowstate at 500 Hz.
