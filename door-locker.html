Automatic Door Locker (Locki)

actuator NEMA 17 Stepper + TMC2209 controller Raspberry Pi Pico (RP2040) gear reduction Compound Spur (5.4:1) CAD SolidWorks 2024

A universal retrofit smart lock designed to mount directly over standard deadbolt turn knobs without modifying the door. Combines a 3D-printed high-torque gear train, custom Altium power circuitry, and MicroPython firmware with sensorless stall homing.

[01/08] CAD Assembly — Full enclosure with custom deadbolt adapter
  • Engineered a high-torque compound gear train in SolidWorks delivering 5.2 Nm stall torque to reliably actuate stiff commercial deadbolts.
  • Routed a custom 2-layer PCB in Altium integrating an LM2596S buck regulator, INA219 current monitor, and TMC2209 silent stepper driver.
  • Implemented sensorless stall detection on the RP2040 Pico to automatically detect mechanical end-stops without noisy physical limit switches.
