Airfoil Wind Tunnel

instrumentation Orthogonal 2-Axis Load Cells controller Raspberry Pi + HX711 24-bit ADC airflow Variable Speed Duct + Flow Straightener team WatArrow Student Design Team

A closed-jet aerodynamic test tunnel engineered for the WatArrow flight design team to measure lift and drag polars on experimental airfoils. Features modular load-cell instrumentation, automated angle-of-attack pitch control, and continuous data acquisition.

[01/08] How: Modeled the wind tunnel in CAD, fabricated components via CNC and 3D printing, then integrated a stepper-motor drive, load cells, and wiring to a Raspberry Pi for data capture and analysis.
  • Designed full wind tunnel assembly in SolidWorks with quick-disconnect modular airfoil mounts and orthogonal load-cell isolation.
  • Engineered a 2-axis force measurement rig capable of resolving aerodynamic forces down to 0.05 N using calibrated strain-gauge load cells.
  • Developed Python DAQ software with live plotting to log lift-to-drag curves across varying angles of attack.
