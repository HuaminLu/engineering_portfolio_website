WatArrow RC Aircraft

competition SAE Aero East 2025 (Micro Class) structure Balsa Ribs + Carbon Fiber Layup award 3rd Place Mission Points avionics Custom Flight Computer + Telemetry

A competition-grade radio-controlled aircraft engineered for maximum payload-to-empty-weight ratio in the SAE Aero Design East Micro Class. Features modular interchangeable balsa wings, wet-layup carbon fiber landing gear, and high-reliability servo wiring harnesses.

[01/08] How: Prototyped foam-cut control surfaces and fabricated CNC-cut balsa wing ribs with heat-shrink laminate. Drafted electrical schematics and wired servos to the flight computer with banana-plug harnesses. Debugged a flap-creep issue during the competition, helping team to secure 3rd-place in mission points.
  • Manufactured modular CNC-cut balsa wings and performed custom vacuum-bagged carbon fiber wet layup for high-impact landing gear.
  • Wired and routed modular flight computer harness with crimped strain-relieved connections, eliminating in-flight voltage dips.
  • Diagnosed and resolved an in-field flight computer firmware glitch during competition, securing 3rd Place in Mission Points.
