Arm Recorder GUI — 14-DOF Joint-Space Trajectory Animator

control LowCmd_ @ 50 Hz joints 14 arm + 3 waist gains kp=60 / kd=1.5 topic rt/arm_sdk

A desktop GUI to dial, record, and replay multi-joint arm trajectories on the G1's two 7-DOF arms — full position/velocity/torque/stiffness streaming over DDS. The recordings become training data for a continuous arm policy.

  • Dial, record, and stream 14 arm and 3 waist joints simultaneously at 50 Hz over CycloneDDS.
  • Hardware startup seeding reads live encoder telemetry from rt/lowstate to prevent zero-snap.
  • Real-time kinematic mirroring flips chiral axes to command symmetrical dual-arm motions.
