I'm a 2B Mechatronics Engineering student at the University of Waterloo who builds robots end-to-end — SolidWorks CAD, power and communication electronics, and embedded firmware in C++ and Python.

I've built a 7-DOF quasi-direct-drive robotic arm with a ~1:1 payload-to-weight ratio, a 10-servo dexterous hand, and a suite of control systems for a 29-DOF humanoid robot — reinforcement-learning arm control, real-time hand teleoperation, and bipedal locomotion.

My interests: humanoid and industrial robotics, learned control, and hardware that survives the real world.

Currently looking for robotics, mechanical design, and hardware engineering co-op roles.
