Cryptex Storage Cylinder

mechanism Rotating Tumbler + Locking Gate dials 3 Independent Combination Rings tolerances ±0.15 mm Rotational Clearance CAD SolidWorks Multi-Body

A mechanical combination locking cylinder with internal notched tumblers and alignment gates. Engineered as a pure mechanisms challenge to achieve silky, tactile rotation and secure interlocking purely through 3D printed mechanical tolerances.

[01/06] Cryptex Cylinder Assembly — Custom mechanical cryptex-style lid with locking pins
  • Modeled a complex multi-body combination mechanism in SolidWorks with interlocking notched pins and alignment gates.
  • Tuned 3D printing slicing parameters and tolerances to ±0.15 mm, eliminating binding while preventing code deciphering by feel.
  • Designed tactile click detents on each outer ring to provide positive user feedback during combination entry.
