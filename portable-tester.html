Portable PCB Tester Tool

compute Raspberry Pi 5 + Custom Interface battery Integrated LiPo + Fuel Gauge enclosure Layered Modular 3D Chassis application Field Flashing & Telematics QA

A rugged, handheld diagnostic and firmware flashing tool built for field engineers and warehouse technicians. Packages a Raspberry Pi 5, touch display, battery power management, and custom breakout harnesses into a compact, impact-resistant 3D-printed enclosure.

[01/08] How: Designed an enclosure in CAD with compartments for the screen, battery, ports, and test board. Assembled the tool with 3D printing and organized internal wiring to components. Programmed a GUI on a Raspberry Pi 5 embedded in the tool to streamline firmware flashing and device diagnostics during field use.
  • Designed a multi-layer modular enclosure in CAD with internal thermal ventilation, recessed port protection, and battery isolation.
  • Built an integrated power system with battery state-of-charge LED indicators, USB-C PD charging, and hot-swap capabilities.
  • Streamlined firmware flashing workflows from 15 minutes down to under 45 seconds with one-touch automated Python scripts.

01Mechanical Design & CAD Assembly

Comprehensive SolidWorks 3D CAD modeling, multi-body kinematics, tolerance stack-up analysis, and assembly architecture.

01.1 Handheld Field Tester Architecture & Ergonomic CAD

Compact, rugged handheld diagnostic tool CAD assembly designed for single-operator field testing of automated sorting equipment.

[01/03] Full Assembly: CAD model of the portable tester tool, designed as a compact all-in-one device for field use.

01.2 Modular Sliding Port Covers & Dust Seals

Sliding dust covers and flush-mount bezels protecting Raspberry Pi 5 USB ports, HDMI debug outputs, battery charging, and wire harness connectors.

[01/05] Port Cutout Covers: Raspberry Pi 5 USB ports, battery interface, and harness connector all feature precise cutouts and sliding covers that mount cleanly to the enclosure for a polished, integrated design.

01.3 Stacked Multi-Layer Packaging & Electronics Bays

Four-tier modular internal skeleton separating touchscreen display, Raspberry Pi compute board, test interface PCB, and battery power supply.

[01/04] Layered Component Assembly: Exploded view showing organized component layers with dedicated spaces for electronics and wire routing, enabling a clean and compact internal layout.

02Prototyping, 3D Printing & Fabrication

Rapid prototyping iterations, slicing optimization (Bambu Studio / PrusaSlicer), material selection (PLA / PETG / Carbon Fiber), and precision fit checks.

02.1 Multi-Layer Internal Frame Prototyping

Additive manufacturing of stacked intermediate frames to evaluate tolerance stack-up and verify rigid screw anchor points.

[01/05] • 3D Printed Layers: Printed prototype layer frames and to separate components and test fit and tolerancing.

02.2 Custom Sliding Port Covers & ABS Enclosure Fit

Prototyping sliding cover tracks and test-fitting stacked printed frames within the outer ABS protective shell.

[01/05] • 3D Printed Port Covers: Printed cover prototypes to test fit and alignment around USB and harness ports.

02.3 Internal Cable Channeling & Component Trays

Benchtop test routing of flat flex ribbons, power cables, and test signals through dedicated isolation channels in the sub-chassis.

[01/07] • Wiring & Electronics Layout: Tested wiring paths and electronics placement on printed layers to validate fit.

02.4 Prototype V1 Iteration & Tkinter Software Bring-Up

Initial operational V1 unit with Raspberry Pi 4 running Ubuntu, Tkinter GUI, and Dremeled enclosure ports for functional proof-of-concept.

[01/06] • Portable Tester v1: Featured LCD screen and hard dremeled USB port cutouts, mainly a prototype design.

02.5 Sliding Harness Door & Port Cover Track

Upgrading from rough Dremel cutouts to smooth injection-quality sliding doors with detents for cable harness mating.

[01/06] • Design Choice: Version 2 added port covers for a cleaner look, upgraded to a glass touchscreen with Raspberry Pi 5, and included an HDMI port to help debug PCBs on the big screen, as the tool is also a computer. Precision port covers for USB-A, USB-C, HDMI, and integrated LED battery fuel gauge windows with power toggle switch bezels.

Side-by-side comparison of the rough V1 prototype versus the sleek, industrial-grade V2 tool featuring Raspberry Pi 5 and Windows 11 compatibility.
